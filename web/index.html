About this prototype & its limits+

This prototype supports one complete, clearly visible circular ring beside the supplied marker, on a plain, light, matte surface. Use a sharp photo, even light and a near-overhead view. Keep both objects on the same plane and leave space between them.

Reflective or very pale rings, strong shadows, stones obscuring the opening, patterned backgrounds, tilted or deformed rings, multiple objects and significant lens distortion can make measurement unreliable. A correctly printed and independently checked marker is essential.

Validation status: the included examples are synthetic engineering fixtures. They test the processing flow; they do not establish accuracy on real photographs. Physical validation with at least two independently measured objects is still required. Estimated uncertainty is provisional.

All image processing runs locally in your browser with OpenCV.js. There are no paid recognition, reasoning or upload operations per photo. Hosting and bandwidth are separate from the displayed API cost.

A GOOD PHOTO MAKES THE DIFFERENCE

Set up for a better estimate.

  1. 01

    Print the actual-size marker

    Download the SVG and print at 100% / actual size. Turn off “fit to page”. Check that the black square’s outside edges measure exactly 20 × 20 mm with a ruler.

  2. 02

    Lay everything flat

    Use one ring with a clearly visible opening on a plain, light background. Put the marker next to it on the same surface, without touching or overlapping.

  3. 03

    Photograph from directly above

    Keep the phone parallel to the surface. Use soft, even light, avoid shadows and reflections, and make sure both objects are fully in frame and in focus.

  4. 04

    Upload your finished photograph

    Choose a JPG, PNG or WebP. We’ll detect the reference and the ring automatically. Your photo stays on your device.
